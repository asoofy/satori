## Formulas to know (also on the reference sheet)

- Rectangle $\ell w$, triangle $\frac12 bh$, circle $\pi r^2$.
- Prism $\ell w h$, cylinder $\pi r^2 h$, cone $\frac13 \pi r^2 h$, sphere $\frac43 \pi r^3$, sphere surface $4\pi r^2$.

## Scaling

If every length is multiplied by $k$, **area** is multiplied by $k^2$ and **volume** by $k^3$.

## Working backward

Given the volume or surface area, solve for the length first, then compute what's asked. A cube with surface area 54 has faces of area 9, so edge 3 and volume 27.

## Trap

Radius vs. diameter: halve the diameter before squaring.

## Angles

- A straight line is $180^\circ$ (supplementary). Vertical angles are equal.
- Parallel lines cut by a transversal: alternate interior and corresponding angles are **equal**; same-side interior angles add to $180^\circ$.
- A triangle's angles add to $180^\circ$. An isosceles triangle has two equal base angles.

## Similar triangles

Corresponding sides are proportional. A segment parallel to one side of a triangle creates a smaller similar triangle.

## Worked example

$\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}$, $AD = 4$, $DB = 6$, $DE = 5$: the scale factor is $\frac{AB}{AD} = \frac{10}{4}$, so $BC = 12.5$. Use the **whole** side $AB$, not $DB$.

## Pythagorean theorem

$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$, where $c$ is the hypotenuse. Common triples: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25 (and their multiples).

## Special right triangles

- $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$: $x,\ x\sqrt3,\ 2x$.
- $45^\circ$-$45^\circ$-$90^\circ$: $s,\ s,\ s\sqrt2$.

## Trig ratios (from angle $A$)

$\sin A = \frac{\text{opp}}{\text{hyp}}$, $\cos A = \frac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}}$, $\tan A = \frac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}$.

For complementary angles, $\sin x^\circ = \cos(90 - x)^\circ$.

## Worked example

$\cos P = \frac{8}{17}$: the opposite side is $\sqrt{17^2 - 8^2} = 15$, so $\tan P = \frac{15}{8}$.

## Arcs and sectors

A central angle $\theta$ takes the fraction $\frac{\theta}{360}$ of the whole circle:

- Arc length $= \frac{\theta}{360} \cdot 2\pi r$. Sector area $= \frac{\theta}{360} \cdot \pi r^2$.
- Radians: $180^\circ = \pi$. To convert degrees to radians, multiply by $\frac{\pi}{180}$.

## Equation of a circle

$(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$ has center $(h, k)$ and radius $r$.

From $x^2 + y^2 + Dx + Ey + F = 0$, **complete the square**:

$$x^2 + 6x + y^2 - 4y = 12 \;\Rightarrow\; (x+3)^2 + (y-2)^2 = 25$$

Center $(-3, 2)$, radius $5$. Watch the signs: $(x + 3)$ means $h = -3$.
