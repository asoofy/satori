title: How to Use Desmos on the SAT
summary: Solve equations, systems and data questions faster with the built-in graphing calculator.
---
The digital SAT includes a Desmos graphing calculator for the whole Math section. In Satori, open it with the **Calculator** button on any Math question.

## 1. Solve an equation by graphing

Type each side as its own line and find where they meet:

- Type `3(x-4)+5` and then `20` on separate lines.
- Click the intersection point. Its $x$-coordinate is the solution.

Alternatively, type the whole equation `3(x-4)+5=20`, and Desmos draws a vertical line at the answer.

## 2. Systems of equations

Type both equations exactly as given (`6x-4y=9`, `3x+2y=12`) and click the intersection. Parallel lines mean no solution. Lines that sit on top of each other mean infinitely many.

## 3. Zeros, vertex, maximum and minimum

Type the function, e.g. `y=-16x^2+64x+5`. Desmos marks the zeros and the vertex with gray dots; click one to see its coordinates.

## 4. Sliders for unknown constants

If a question has a constant like $k$, type `y=x^2+kx+25` and add a slider for `k`. Drag it until the condition holds, for example when the parabola just touches the $x$-axis (one solution).

## 5. Statistics

Type a list like `L=[12,7,19,4,15,10]`, then `mean(L)`, `median(L)` or `stdev(L)`. For regression, make a table and type `y1 ~ m x1 + b` to get the line of best fit.

## 6. Check your algebra

Graph the original expression and your simplified version. If the graphs are identical, the expressions are equivalent.

## When not to use it

Quick arithmetic and simple one-step equations are faster by hand. Use Desmos when the algebra is messy or you want to check an answer.

## Pacing

- Reading and Writing: 27 questions in 32 minutes, about **70 seconds** each.
- Math: 22 questions in 35 minutes, about **95 seconds** each.
- If a question is taking twice that long, **mark it for review**, guess, and move on.

## Never leave a blank

There's no penalty for wrong answers. Before time runs out, answer every question, even with a guess.

## Module 1 matters most

Module 2 is harder or easier depending on how you do in module 1, and the easier route limits your top score. Give module 1 your full focus.

## Use the tools

- Cross out choices you've ruled out.
- Highlight the claim in evidence questions.
- Use the review page to find unanswered and marked questions before submitting.

## Practice like it's real

Take full-length practice tests under timed conditions, then spend at least as long reviewing every miss as you spent taking the test.
